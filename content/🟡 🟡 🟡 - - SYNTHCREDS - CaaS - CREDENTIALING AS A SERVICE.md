### 1. THEY LOSE THE GATEKEEPER TOLLBOOTH

In the legacy world, if an alumnus needs to prove their credential to an employer in Canada or a multinational company in Vietnam, the employer has to go back to the school's portal, order an official transcript, or pay a verification fee. The school controls the gate.

With **SYNCTESS** and **SYNTHCREDs**:

- The school's database becomes a passive archive. They can look at it all they want, but they can no longer charge a toll or force people back into their closed ecosystem to prove it's real.
    

### 2. THE LEGACY TRAP (THE ASYMMETRY OF MOVING BACK)

Say the Guild gets frustrated after a year of learner sovereignty and decides to cancel your CaaS contract. They want to move to a legacy, centralized vendor that promises to lock everything back down in a proprietary portal.

To revoke a sovereign credential, the issuer must publish a cryptographic revocation state that networks like the Skyway check against.
